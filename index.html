Help

❓ How to Sell?

Log in as a Farmer, go to "Sell Harvest", fill the form and post it. Your listing appears in the marketplace right away.

🎤 How to Use Voice Search?

Press the microphone button and say the crop name and place, like "Find tomato near Pune".

💰 How to Check Prices?

Open "Market Prices" to compare the reference price with farmer and buyer prices.

🤝 How to Contact Buyers?

On any listing, press "Contact Farmer" or "Send Request" to reach out directly.

📷 How to Upload Crop?

In the Sell Harvest form, press "Upload Crop Photo" and choose a clear picture of your crop.

📞 Contact Support

Call our helpline at 1800-000-000 (demo number) for assistance in your language.
